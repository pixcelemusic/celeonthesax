Celeste De Vito

Saxophonist, Composer

Celeste De Vito is a saxophonist and composer based in Kitchener Ontario. She is finishing a bachelor of arts degree at Wilfrid Laurier, having studied with Dave Wiffen, Adrean Ferrugia, and Kevin Day. On top of her performances with Laurier's Jazz Orchestra, she has played alongside Amanda Braam and the Paper Cranes at both the Jazz Room and Waterloo's Canada Day event.

She composes for a variety of mediums, with pieces of hers being performed by the Laurier Jazz Orchestra and featured in workshops ran by the KW Symphony. She also experiments in electronic ambient styles.
